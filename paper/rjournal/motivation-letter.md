Please consider this article titled "bayprior: Structured Bayesian Prior
Elicitation, Conflict Diagnostics, and Regulatory Reporting" for
publication in the R Journal.

The manuscript introduces bayprior, an R package and Shiny application
that provides a structured workflow for Bayesian prior specification and
justification in clinical trials, addressing a gap left by existing
packages such as RBesT, trialr, and hdbayes, which focus on posterior
computation rather than prior construction and validation.

I believe readers of the R Journal will find this article useful, as
the FDA's 2026 draft guidance on Bayesian methods makes structured prior
documentation a live regulatory requirement, and bayprior is, to my
knowledge, the first integrated R package addressing the full prior
elicitation, conflict diagnostic, sensitivity, and regulatory reporting
workflow.
